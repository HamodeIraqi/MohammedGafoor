Executive Summary
A seasoned technology leader and enterprise systems architect with over 22 years of experience in Iraq's sovereign government sector. Since 2022, he has directed the IT Department of the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers, overseeing the nation's core digital infrastructure and comprehensive automation initiatives. As a Full-Stack Architect, he has deep expertise in designing and building large-scale systems and sensitive database platforms. His career is highlighted by the successful launch of landmark strategic platforms — including the Iraqi Government E-Mail System and the Council of Ministers Agenda Management System — and is crowned by multiple high-level Letters of Appreciation from three successive Iraqi Prime Ministers.

Key Strategic Projects & Systems Delivered

Iraqi Government E-Mail System
The unified national platform for official correspondence between the Prime Minister's Office, ministries, and governorates — secured with the highest encryption standards.
Council of Ministers Agenda System
The electronic system for managing decisions, agendas, and deliberations of Cabinet sessions, enabling digital and confidential circulation of ministerial memos.
Kurdistan Region Representation System
A coordination and direct digital communication platform for managing shared issues and official correspondence between the Federal Government and the Region.
Iraqi Companies System (Public & Private)
A comprehensive database and archiving system for tracking the status of companies and investment projects across Iraq.
Sovereign Documents Archiving System
An enterprise archiving system for managing, preserving, and retrieving millions of documents, Diwan orders, and meeting minutes electronically.
Official Secretariat General Portal
Development and launch of the central e-portal with advanced security levels to deliver news, services, and official data to institutions and the public.
